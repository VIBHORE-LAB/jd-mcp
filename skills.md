# AI Cover Letter & Application Skill Rules

## Humanization & Voice Guidelines
- Write like an authentic professional speaking directly to another professional, avoiding generic AI templates.
- Strictly ban overused AI clichés and buzzwords (e.g., "delighted", "thrilled", "beacon", "testament", "synergy", "spearheaded", "tapestry", "fast-paced environment", "humbled").
- Use conversational, direct opening hooks explaining why this specific role or company caught your attention.
- Show, don't just tell: link achievements to concrete user impact and production metrics.
- Keep sentence lengths varied for natural reading flow and rhythm.

## Application Question Response Strategy
- Answer questions directly in first-person ("I").
- Ground answers in actual technical accomplishments and work experience from profile.md.
- Maintain a problem-solver mindset: explain the challenge, your specific engineering action, and the outcome.

## Formatting & Output Guidelines
- Structure cover letters cleanly: Contact Header, Date, Recipient Header, Opening Hook, Impact Paragraph, Strategic Fit Bullets, Call to Action Closing.
- Ensure all text is immediately exportable to clean printable PDF.
